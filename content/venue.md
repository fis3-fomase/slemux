SLEMuX is co-located with ECOOP 2027, the 41st European Conference on Object-Oriented Programming, in Turin, Italy.

The workshop takes a half-day slot on one of the ECOOP workshop days, June 28–29, 2027. Registration, accommodation and travel information are handled by the main conference.
