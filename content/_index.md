Robot swarms, IoT and edge–cloud systems, decentralised applications, socio-technical platforms, and networks of LLM-based agents are all made of many interacting computational entities, and what matters most is what they do collectively.

SLEMuX brings together the programming-language communities that address the micro–macro link — macroprogramming and aggregate computing, choreographic and multiparty languages, multi-tier, agent-oriented, organisational and holonic programming, neuro-symbolic and differentiable programming — to identify common foundations, language designs, type systems, semantics, implementation techniques, and tools.
